## How to build a quote in 90 seconds

1. Pick the **Product** (Reyansh Outdoor / Indoor, Leynna Cosmo, Leynna Sapphire) + **Pixel Pitch**.
2. Enter **Width × Height in feet**. Tool auto-snaps to whole cabinets.
3. Look at the **Recommended** or **Nearest** fit card — click one to lock it in.
4. Fill **Project name** + **Client** in the top strip.
5. Hit **↓ Documents → Quote PDF** — a print-ready page opens; use browser Print to save as PDF.
6. Share the PDF with your customer.
